# Ainu language research — 7 September 2026

Targeted survey of 2025–2026 publications in Ainu linguistics, textual studies, and language revitalization. Sources checked include HUSCAP, ACL Anthology, Wiener Linguistische Gazette, Cascadilla Proceedings Project, the CELEA4 abstract book, and the Hungarian Scientific Bibliography (MTMT), John Benjamins, Crossref, Studia Orientalia, the University of Helsinki, Hokkaido Museum, and the Hokkaido Museum of Northern Peoples. Publication years were checked against repository metadata or the publications themselves; search-engine crawl dates were not treated as publication dates. This survey is not an exhaustive repository harvest.
